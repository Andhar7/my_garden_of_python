  📖 PART 1: What is CORRELATION? (With Russian Translation)

  Simple Explanation

  Correlation = How two things MOVE TOGETHER

  Russian: "Корреляция" (Korrelyatsiya)

  Examples to Understand

  POSITIVE Correlation (+):
  - More study hours → Higher exam scores ✅
  - Both move in SAME direction
  - Both go UP together

  NEGATIVE Correlation (-):
  - More exercise → Lower weight ✅
  - They move in OPPOSITE directions
  - One goes UP, other goes DOWN

  NO Correlation (0):
  - Shoe size → Intelligence ❌
  - No relationship at all
  - Random

  The Number Scale (-1.0 to +1.0)

  -1.0 _____________ 0 _____________ +1.0
  Perfect   Moderate   Weak    None    Weak   Moderate  Perfect
  Negative  Negative Negative        Positive Positive Positive

  Example values:
  -0.95 = Very strong opposite (if one ↑, other strongly ↓)
  -0.50 = Moderate opposite
   0.00 = No relationship at all
  +0.50 = Moderate together
  +0.95 = Very strong together (if one ↑, other strongly ↑)
